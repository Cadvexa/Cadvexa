Capabilities

A practical extension of your project team.

Cadvexa is built to take defined drafting and documentation requirements off your team's workload and return organized, usable drawing output.

Cadvexa supports architecture, construction and engineering workflows with CAD and documentation services across different stages of a project.

You can provide existing drawings, markups, sketches, PDFs, references or project requirements. We work from the information provided, clarify the scope and produce the required drafting output.
