Capabilities

Extend your project team without expanding it permanently.

Cadvexa supports defined CAD and documentation requirements so your team can add production capacity when project workload demands it.

Project teams do not always need another permanent employee. Sometimes they need reliable drafting capacity for a specific stage, drawing package or recurring workflow.
