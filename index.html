How an engagement works

Our approach

Designed to integrate with your existing team.

Cadvexa is a managed production-support service. The goal is to help your team handle defined drafting and documentation tasks without making your workflow harder.

We start by understanding the task, the source information, your standards and the intended use of the deliverables. If the brief has gaps, those should be clarified before a quote or deadline is treated as final.

A straightforward process is more valuable than a long list of unverified claims.

We do not publish client names, project outcomes, certifications or portfolio examples unless they are genuine and we have permission to share them.
